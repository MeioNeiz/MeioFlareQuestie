# FlareQuestie

## 1.2.0

- First release: Questie's data in FlareUI's quest tracker
- Yards to each quest beside its name, the nearest in white; Sort: Distance by the nearest objective
- Questie's usable quest items as buttons, "Return to" the turn-in NPC on finished quests
- Show on Map (or Alt-click a quest) opens the map and flashes the quest's Questie icons
- Quest tooltips: nearest objective or turn-in NPC with yards and direction; Shift for level, elite or group tags and clicks
- Quest count in the header turns orange at 2 free log slots, red when the log is full
- Search: the magnifier in the header lists only quests whose name, zone, objectives or turn-in NPC match
- Resize grip, compact spacing (off by default), settings in Esc > Options > AddOns
